Darmanin says Le Pen or Mélenchon as president would end Europe as it exists

The French justice minister made the claim at a Barcelona forum, backing Édouard Philippe for 2027 and calling for a pro-European candidate who would turn Europe into one of power rather than discussion.

6 November 2026

Justice Minister Gérald Darmanin said on 2 November at a Barcelona forum that Marine Le Pen or Jean-Luc Mélenchon would kill Europe as it exists. He said this would happen if either wins the 2027 presidential election. He called instead for a very pro-European candidate who would transform Europe into a Europe of power rather than a Europe of discussion.

Darmanin appeared in conversation with Spanish Foreign Minister José Manuel Albares on justice, multilateralism and Europe's political future. Albares called for a European digital market, saying it was needed to guard against external interference. He also advocated a European capital market and a community army, which he argued need not weaken NATO.

In Barcelona he called on European states to rearm autonomously. He noted that French debates often dwell on pensions, health insurance and end of life, while the world discusses quantum physics, artificial intelligence and climate. France, he said, should project itself toward those of the future.

Darmanin supports Édouard Philippe, a candidate in the 2027 presidential election. If Le Pen wins, he said, Europe must defend itself.

Darmanin said it was undeniable that under either candidate Europe as it exists today would no longer exist. There would still be a European continent and countries talking to each other, but no European Community.
